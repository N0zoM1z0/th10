## Scratch policy

Session scratch belongs under `build/claude/` and `.analysis/claude/` (or an
agent's own subdirectory) and is deleted once its findings are in
`KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md` or `RE_HANDOFF.md`. `build/probe-ltcg/` and
`build/probe-backlog/` are regenerated by their scripts. Every
`build/match-linked/` directory is the output of a manifest unit and can be
rebuilt from `config/match-units.toml`.
